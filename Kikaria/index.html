Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Data Collection

Kikaria does not collect, upload, sell, or share users' personal data.

Local Data

Study content, collections, progress, and app settings are stored locally on the user's device.

Photos

If the user chooses to set a profile avatar, Kikaria may access the photo selected by the user. The selected image is used only inside the app and is not uploaded to any server.

Third-Party Services

Kikaria does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud data collection services in the current version.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact: qiji070818@gmail.com

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 5 月 10 日

数据收集

Kikaria 不收集、不上传、不出售、也不共享用户的个人数据。

本地数据

用户的学习内容、重点集锦、学习进度和应用设置均保存在用户设备本地。

照片

如果用户选择设置个人头像，Kikaria 可能会访问用户主动选择的照片。该照片仅用于应用内头像显示，不会上传至任何服务器。

第三方服务

当前版本的 Kikaria 不使用第三方分析、广告、追踪或云端数据收集服务。

联系方式
